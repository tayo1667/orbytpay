Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about OrbytPay services

How secure are my transactions with OrbytPay?

OrbytPay uses bank-level security with 256-bit SSL encryption, multi-factor authentication, and real-time fraud monitoring. All transactions are protected by multiple layers of security protocols that exceed international banking standards.

Which African countries does OrbytPay support?

OrbytPay operates across all 54 African countries, supporting local currencies, payment methods, and regulatory requirements in each nation. We're continuously expanding our services to better serve the African continent.

What are the transaction fees?

Our fees are transparent and competitive. Domestic transfers start from 0.5%, international transfers from 1.2%, and crypto exchanges from 0.8%. Use our pricing calculator above for exact fees based on your transaction details.

How long do transactions take to process?

Most transactions are processed instantly or within 2 seconds. International transfers may take 1-3 business days depending on the destination country and local banking regulations.

Can I use OrbytPay for business transactions?

Yes! OrbytPay offers comprehensive business solutions including merchant services, payroll management, bulk payments, API integration, and dedicated business support. Contact our business team for customized solutions.

Is customer support available 24/7?

Yes, our customer support team is available 24/7 through live chat, phone, and email. We provide support in over 20 African languages to ensure you can get help in your preferred language.
